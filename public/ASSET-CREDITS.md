# Globe and interface artwork

- `earth-day-5400.jpg`: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation, August 2004 with topography. Original 5400 × 2700 image, used without geographic alterations. https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography/
- `earth-clouds.jpg`: Solar System Scope / INOVE, Earth Clouds, CC BY 4.0. Used as a translucent cloud layer. https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/ and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- `earth-normal.jpg`, `earth-specular.jpg`: three.js example planet textures. https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures/planets
- `tabletop-sunlight.png`, `porcelain-plate.png`: generated specifically for Safe Serve. The tabletop is 1672 × 941 and the plate is 1254 × 1254; neither is described as native 4K.
- `meal-bowls.png`: generated with the built-in image-generation tool for the reference-based meal carousel on September 9, 2026. Transparent 1254 × 1254 sprite sheet. Prompt: six ceramic food bowls in a three-column, two-row grid (Mexican tacos, Asian tofu noodles, Mediterranean chicken grain salad, Italian spaghetti, Middle Eastern mezze, healthy salmon bowl), consistent camera angle, soft golden light from upper right, restrained natural colors, no text or background. The wooden platforms are styled separately using the existing tabletop texture.

## Restaurant variety atlas

`restaurant-variety-atlas.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-18. Twelve illustrative scenes are selected as CSS atlas tiles; they do not depict or certify the listed restaurants.

Prompt: “Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create one photographic atlas for illustrative restaurant card backgrounds: precisely 4 columns by 3 rows, twelve equal rectangular panels, no gaps, no borders, no text. Each panel is a different high quality editorial food or restaurant stock photo: row 1 sunny Mediterranean terrace, overhead colorful rice bowl, cozy brick cafe interior, rustic tomato pasta; row 2 Japanese dining room, grilled vegetables on ceramic plate, elegant candlelit bistro, tacos with corn tortillas; row 3 bright bakery counter, seafood and lemon plate, modern green courtyard restaurant, colorful Indian curry with rice. Warm natural colors, real textures, diverse camera angles, no logos or recognizable businesses, no people in focus. These are illustrative images, not actual restaurant evidence. Landscape atlas 1536 by 1024 or larger, evenly divided into 4x3 tiles.”
