GF + ALLERGY WORLD CITY INDEX — 2026 COMPACT EDITION
Coverage: 40 cities. Food: 5 leads each for B/C/D/N/P/S. Allergy: 3 leads each for ML/PN/SF.

READ FIRST — SAFETY
This is a travel-planning shortlist, not a medical guarantee. “Allergy-aware,” “vegan,” “dedicated GF,” and “no listed allergen” do not prove zero cross-contact. Recipes, suppliers, branches, fryers and staff procedures change. Before ordering: name every allergy and its severity; confirm the exact dish; ask about shared oil/woks/grills, utensils, marinades, stocks, sauces, soy sauce and toppings; leave if staff cannot answer. Raw-fish venues commonly handle shellfish. Peanut-free diners should avoid venues with loose peanuts or uncontrolled peanut oil.

SORT + FORMAT
Cities A→Z. Food fields: B=burger; C=chicken; D=dessert/bakery; N=noodles/pasta/ramen/pho; P=pizza; S=sushi/rolls. Food ranks run 1→5. Allergy fields: ML=milk/dairy; PN=peanut; SF=shellfish. Allergy ranks run 1→3. N includes Asian noodles and Italian pasta. Repeated venues are intentional when they fit several filters.

STATUS LEGEND
[D]=reported dedicated gluten-free kitchen/facility; [A]=published allergen or coeliac process/menu reported; [M]=GF option in a shared kitchen; [V]=vegan venue/menu used as a candidate (does not itself prove GF or allergy safety); [?]=unverified lead—do not rely on it until the branch confirms. Allergy tags: [AM]=published allergen matrix/process candidate; [Q]=phone-first candidate. PN lists favor allergen tools/processes but the venues may still use peanuts. ML/SF lists favor vegan venues to reduce ingredient risk, not cross-contact risk. Every tag is provisional and must be rechecked.

RANKING
Order is an editorial shortlist: reputation/popularity → taste → allergy handling. It is not a measured global ranking. Original B/P/D order is retained where possible; new entries are candidate order. If a venue cannot confirm the requested dish and precautions, skip it and move to the next lead.
